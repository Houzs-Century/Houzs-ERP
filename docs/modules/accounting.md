# Accounting

Houzs's own general ledger, kept to formal-book standard while AutoCount runs in parallel (the ERP pushes documents to AutoCount).
Covers the posting engine, chart of accounts, finance documents (AP invoices, AR invoices, Other Debtors, receipts, credit/debit notes, deposit invoices, official receipts), merchant and bank reconciliation, daily and month-end closes, and the financial reports.
Used by Finance and the owner on desktop, through the Finance sidebar (Money in, Money out, Bank & cards, Books, Reports, Setup).
Payment vouchers have their own guide (`docs/modules/payment-voucher.md`); the requirements brief is `docs/新ERP会计模块需求书.md`.

## Statuses and flow

Journal entries
- Every entry is written POSTED by the engine; a manual journal (JV) starts DRAFT and posts via `POST /accounting/journal-entries/:id/post`.
- A reversal is a contra entry, never a delete: the original carries `reversed`, the contra `reversed_by_je`. Manual journals reverse via `/reverse`; document entries only through their document's own cancel.
- Editing a manual journal (`PUT /journal-entries/:id`): validate, write the corrected DRAFT, reverse the old entry with a contra dated the OLD entry's day, post. A DRAFT is rewritten in place; document entries refuse `not_manual`, reversed ones `already_reversed`.
- Journal class (SALES / PURCHASE / BANK / CASH / GENERAL) is derived by `classifyJournal`, never stored; SOPAY/SIPAY/PV split CASH vs BANK by the money account they touch.

Auto-posting source types (reversal = `<TYPE>_REVERSAL`)
- `SI` sales invoice: Dr AR (customer party) / Cr each item group's sales account; posts at create/confirm, resync voids and re-posts after post-issue edits.
- `PI` purchase invoice: Dr each item group's purchase account / Cr the supplier's AP control (400 or 405). `API` AP invoice: Dr each line's account / Cr AP control.
- `PV` payment voucher: posts at approve, reverses at cancel.
- `SOPAY` / `SIPAY` customer payment: Dr cash, default bank or the acquirer's clearing account / Cr AR. `SOCONV` money moved off a cancelled order: Dr AR (old customer) / Cr AR (new customer).
- `CASHUP` daily-close cash over/short (946-0000; corrected by JV). `STOCKADJ` month-end stock pair: Dr 330-0000 / Cr 620-0000 on the last day, mirror dated the 1st.
- `SETTLE` merchant fee at confirm; `SETTLEMOVE` untagged card money to the merchant's own clearing account; `SETTLEADJ` statement charge with no transaction; `SETTLEBANK` payout credit; `SETTLECHARGE` bank charge kept from a payout; `SETTLECARD` card payments a bank credit took in itself; `SETTLEFOR` another company's sale swiped on this terminal.
- `ODB` debtor bill, `ODR` debtor receipt, `RCT` general receipt, `CN` / `DN` / `SCN` / `OCN` notes, `DI` deposit invoice.
- `COMMREL` monthly commission release (owner 2026-11-25; `acc/commission-release.ts` over `acc/commission-release-plan.ts`, Books › Commission release): only with the company's switch on (`acc_company_settings.commission_release_on`); one entry `COMMREL-{co}-{YYYY-MM}` per month from the books' start, on its last day, Dr COMMISSION_EXPENSE (900-C003) / Cr COMMISSION_PREPAID (360-0050), a pair per bill month; a prepaid line's month is the one it names (`OCT'26`, `AUG26`), else its entry's month; each order's share is what that month's Event Commission close paid it (by SO date, scaled to the bill), a month with no close is split by its orders' goods, the books' opening by the goods of the older orders unsold at the start; a share moves as the order's net sales arrive (SIs + DIs − the CNs that contra a DI, over the order total) and what is left when it is cancelled or closed, never before its bill month; the nightly 00:05 run re-posts a month whose lines moved (contra on its own date). The Performance P&L shows the bills for the period's months on the expense in place of the release (`acc/performance-commission.ts`).

Documents
- Debit notes are bills of their own kind (owner 2026-10-28; `doc_kind` 'DN', migration 20261029T1000): a supplier's DN raises what we owe — an AP invoice numbered `{co}SDN-YYMM-NNN`, posted, aged and paid through the AP Payment exactly as one (source API, journal "Supplier debit note …"); an other debtor's DN raises what they owe — a debtor bill numbered `{co}ODN-YYMM-NNN`, received on Receipts (source ODB, journal "Debit note …", prints DEBIT NOTE). `GET /ap-invoices?docKind=DN` and `GET /ar-invoices?docKind=DN` list them alone; the CN / DN pages show them on their Debit notes / Other debtor DN tabs (the bills pages narrowed by `docKind`), the AP / AR Invoices lists label them Supplier DN / Debit note.
- AP invoice: DRAFT → POSTED → PARTIALLY_PAID / PAID (paid by an AP Payment voucher through `scm.settle_api_paid_sen`). Cancel writes `API_REVERSAL`, refused once money is on it (`has_payments`). Editing a posted bill re-posts. The list also mirrors purchase invoices read-only (`kind: 'PI'`).
- Other Debtor account code (owner 2026-11-03; `scm.acc_debtors.code`, migration 20261105T1900): AutoCount style, `305-A001`, unique per company. Filled once: the code AutoCount gave them where the opening import recorded it, else the next number for the first letter of the name. A new debtor's is suggested on the form; it stays editable, a blank clears it. Screens and prints only: never on a journal line (`party_code` stays NULL on ODB, ODR and OCN), and an OCN reads it beside the note as `debtor_code`.
- Other Debtor bill: posts on create (atomic with its journal); PAID when fully knocked off; cancel refused once money received; edit re-posts; prints as an INVOICE to the debtor (description and amount per line, never the account code; total, received, balance due, words; CANCELLED watermark). The registry holds the party's data as a supplier's — TIN, business reg no, contact person, attention, phones, email, fax, four address lines, city, postcode, state, country (`debtor-party.ts`; one pop-out form for New and Edit) — and BILL TO prints the address, attention, phone, email, TIN and reg no. A line with a description and no amount is a text line (no account, `credit_account_code` NULL): it prints, books nothing, never counts in the total; a bill needs at least one money line. The foot prints the company's Other Debtor payment details and terms from Settings › Branding (`debtorPaymentDetails`, `debtorInvoiceTerms`); the Deposit Invoice and the Sales Invoice print the customer set (`customerPaymentDetails`); a blank setting prints nothing.
- Other Debtor receipt: Draft → Prepared → Checked → Approved (approve posts ODR and knocks off bills, clamped at live outstanding). Reject returns to Draft clearing every mark; withdraw only before Checked. From the Receipts page with `postNow: true` it posts in the same call. A POSTED receipt is VOIDED from the Receipts page (`POST /other-debtors/receipts/:id/void`): ODR contra, each ticked bill takes its money back (PAID → POSTED), the receipt CANCELLED; a draft refuses (`not_posted`), twice refuses (`void_twice`).
- AR Invoices page (owner 2026-09-29; `GET /ar-invoices?kind=ALL|SI|ODB`, reads only): the live sales invoices (SENT / PARTIALLY_PAID / PAID / OVERDUE — never DRAFT or CANCELLED) as a read-only mirror linking to the Sales side, beside the Other Debtor bills, one list with a Kind column, a party filter and Print listing. A bill is raised, edited, copied, cancelled and printed THERE (the writes stay on `/other-debtors`); the Other Debtors page is the registry alone; the money is received and voided on the Receipts page. A sales invoice's outstanding is the SI list's own figure: total − paid − the order's deposit applied − the customer credit notes knocked off it (`stampOrderDeposit` stamps `so_deposit_applied_sen` and `credit_notes_sen`). Deposit invoices stay on their own page; credit / debit notes are not on it. Every row opens (owner 2026-10-06): a debtor bill in its pop-out, a sales invoice as the page it prints (`salesInvoicePdfBlob` in an iframe) with Print, Save PDF and its full page; ticked rows print as ONE document, each as its own print, in the table's order (`ar-invoice-print.ts`).
- General receipt (RCT): posts on create; the only undo is VOID (RCT_REVERSAL + CANCELLED). Editing a posted receipt reverses and re-posts on the new date and keeps its number; a void one refuses (`receipt_cancelled`).
- Credit / debit note (CN and DN to a customer, SCN from a supplier, OCN to an other debtor): DRAFT → POSTED → CANCELLED by contra; a posted note is never edited (`not_editable`). A line's `amountSen` must be a JSON integer above 0: `null` (what the screen sent for an amount `Number()` could not read), a string or `true` is refused `bad_line` (400); the form reads amounts with `parseMoneyToSen` ("3,500.00" = RM 3,500.00) and refuses an unreadable one on screen. Two pages (owner 2026-10-28; `GET /credit-notes?side=AR|AP`): Money in › Customer & Other Debtor CN / DN (`/scm/credit-notes` — CN, DN, OCN) and Money out › Supplier & Other Creditor CN / DN (`/scm/supplier-notes` — SCN, its scan; Finance's supplier list includes its Finance-only ones). An OCN names one of the Other Debtors registry (`debtor_id`, active) and every line its own account (`account_required` — no default): Dr each line / Cr the other-debtor control (party ODEBTOR, the debtor's name). It knocks off the debtor's bills as an SCN knocks off the supplier's invoices — ticks planned on the draft, carried out at post, changed any time after (`acc_credit_note_allocations.debtor_bill_id`, moved through `scm.settle_odb_received_sen`; cancel gives back what was applied); AR aging splits it across the bills it ticked. A CN knocks off the customer's sales invoices on the same table (owner 2026-11-03; `sales_invoice_id`, `acc/credit-note-si.ts`) but posts nothing and settles nothing into the invoice: `paid_sen` stays its receipts (the overpayment rule reads it), every reader of the invoice's outstanding subtracts `credit_notes_sen` beside the deposit, the order's deposit spills past what a note took, and `recomputeSiPaid` counts it for the status. The customer is the note's AR party: a customer id alone, any other code (the shared cash debtor 300-C002) with the name, no code by the name; each take is capped at what the invoice owes after its deposit and the other notes. A note answering a deposit invoice — the final invoice's close-out, a refund's, a move's — knocks off nothing (`deposit_note`); a cancelled CN or SI gives its knock-offs back.
- Credit note from a delivery return (backlog A1, 2026-11-09): `GET /credit-notes/from-return/:returnId` (area guard only, like the other reads) gives the return's order, the issued invoice that billed most of its lines, each returned line priced at that billing per unit (`line_total_sen ÷ qty` over the issued — not draft, cancelled or void — invoice lines on the line's DO line) with its units still uncredited, and the notes already raised from it. `POST /credit-notes` with `deliveryReturnId` (CN only, `return_not_this_kind`) links the note (`acc_credit_notes.delivery_return_id`) and every line (`acc_credit_note_lines.delivery_return_item_id` + `qty`, migration `20261110T0200`); the customer and `sales_invoice_id` come from the return, `source_doc_no` defaults to the return number. Refused: a line naming no return line (`return_line_required`), not this return's (`return_line_unknown`), twice (`return_line_twice`), never invoiced (`return_line_not_invoiced`), more units than returned less those on other live notes (`over_return_credit`), more money than the invoice charged for them (`over_invoice_price`), a cancelled return (`return_cancelled`), another company's (`return_unknown`); a return-line on a note with no return (`return_required`). A draft's edit is held to the same rule, its own units not counted. A DB trigger (`acc_credit_note_lines_return_cap`, row lock on the return line) is the backstop for two saves at once and answers 409 `over_return_credit`. A cancelled note gives its units back. The note posts like any CN (Dr RETURN INWARDS / Cr AR); credit notes are not written back to AutoCount (no CN document type in `services/autocount-writeback.ts`), so nothing changes there. Form: Money in › `?form=new&fromReturn=<id>` fills the note; one unit input per line (amount follows the invoiced price), no added lines.
- Deposit invoice (DI): issued and posted per qualifying customer payment; closed by one CN when the order's final invoice posts (`credit_note_id`); cancel needs a reason (row kept); `POST /deposit-invoices/:id/post` retries a journal refused at birth. A company with `acc_company_settings.deposit_cost_pct` (owner 2026-11-26: Houzs, as AutoCount's 34%) also books that share as cost on the DI's entry, Dr DEPOSIT_COST (619-0000) / Cr DEPOSIT_COST_ACCRUAL (410-0080), recorded in `cost_sen`; every CN naming the DI as `source_doc_no` (close-out, refund, move) releases its share, so a deposit taken back in full releases exactly its cost (`acc/deposit-cost.ts`); `scripts/backfill-deposit-invoice-cost.mjs` posts DIs from before the share was set again with it. The page's Issue missing button runs in ONE Worker request (about 15 subrequests an invoice), so a backlog of more than a few dozen — a start day set in the past — is issued with `scripts/issue-missing-deposit-invoices.mjs` on the production runner (plan, `PLAN_ID`, `MAX_PER_MONTH`). It prints A4 in the Sales Invoice's layout (owner 2026-10-06): BILL TO with the order's address, phone and e-mail (`order-address.ts`, the SO print's rule), DEPOSIT DETAILS, one line, the deposit total, the order's total, paid to date by THIS deposit and the balance (`GET /deposit-invoices/sheets?ids=`, at most 200, in the order asked); a row opens the invoice as it prints, with Print and Save PDF. An invoice with a credit note against it (close-out, refund or conversion note) is never cancelled or re-issued: a payment edit or delete re-posts the payment alone and leaves the invoice, the page refuses `invoice_noted`; cancel the final invoice or the refund voucher first.
- Official receipt: DRAFT (`{co}DraftOR-YYMM`) when the payment is recorded → FORMAL when the money is confirmed: cash at once (`{co}COR`), card at merchant-recon confirm, transfer by the manual Confirm money button.
- Merchant settlement line: MATCHED / NEEDS_CONFIRM / UNMATCHED / IGNORED. Confirm posts SETTLE (+ SETTLEMOVE); Undo (`/settlement/rows/:id/unconfirm`) reverses, releases the links and returns to NEEDS_CONFIRM, refused while the report has recorded payout credits.
- Bank statement line `state`: OPEN → POSTED (matched or money received) or IGNORED (incl. duplicates and bank reversal pairs); Undo reopens the whole match group.
- Bank month (company × account × month): open → locked (`/lock`) → unlocked (`/unlock` sets `released_at`; the row stays).
- Daily close: confirming freezes the day's buckets and posts CASHUP; card/transfer differences are recorded, never posted there.
- Forecast P&L (`GET`/`PUT /accounting/forecast`, `GET /accounting/forecast/figures?from&to`; `scm.acc_forecast_pnl`, one row per month per company, `lines` jsonb code → `{ bp }` | `{ amtSen }`): the rows are the P&L's active leaf accounts on the P&L layout; a sales line takes an amount (the month's forecast sales are their sum), every other line a share in basis points or an amount, never both — the share is of the month's sales, except on a purchase account bound to an item group (`scm.acc_item_group_accounts.purchase_account`), whose share is of that group's `sales_account` lines alone (the account carries `basis`; owner 2026-09-29); a whole-grid PUT names the first bad cell and removes absent months; the figures follow the P&L's blocks via the shared `scm/shared/forecast-pnl.ts` (vendored byte-identical); nothing posts. Read = `scm.payment_voucher.post`, write = `scm.payment_voucher.write`. The page is `/scm/forecast` (Finance › Reports › Overall P&L › Forecast; `ForecastPnl.tsx`): months across, the P&L's tree down (an account printed `code · name` on one line, the report trees' own label), two boxes a cell (RM | % sales — key one, the other shows what it implies), category subtotals and the statement's totals, Add month inherits the percentages, Hide / Show empty lines, one Save of the whole grid that scrolls to a refused cell.
- Forecast P&L by supplier (`scm.acc_forecast_pnl.suppliers` jsonb: purchase account code → supplier id of the company or `other` → `{ amtSen }` | `{ bp }`): a purchase account is a Cost of Goods Sold leaf that an item group's purchase invoices post to, or one named "PURCHASE…" (a purchases return left out; `isPurchaseOfChart`); while any of its suppliers has a figure that month the account IS their sum and its own cell is superseded (kept, counted again once the suppliers are cleared); a supplier % is of the account's own basis; the page, `/forecast/figures` and the Dashboard all read `monthFigures(lines, suppliers, accounts)`; the PUT refuses by name a supplier not of the active company and keeps the stored figures when a body carries no `suppliers`; a new month carries the supplier percentages, not their amounts; GET lists the company's suppliers with the purchase accounts their PI / AP invoices hit since the 1st of the month three months back (the rows a purchase line opens to, plus keyed ones, an Add supplier picker and Other).
- Cash Flow P&L forecast (`GET /accounting/reports/cash-flow-forecast?from=YYYY-MM&to=YYYY-MM`, read = `scm.payment_voucher.post`; `accounting-cashflow-forecast.ts` over `acc/cashflow-pnl-forecast.ts`): each month of the range — and, when it reaches the current month, every later month the money reaches (a year at most) — is the Forecast P&L as cash laid on the Cash Flow's own tree (one money column `FORECAST`; its entries name the forecast month and the timing): sales by the last 3 complete months' customer receipts against their order month, as Deposit / Settlement on the debtor control (imported, converted and mirror payments left out); a supplier-split purchase after each supplier's `payment_terms` (COD, Advance Payment, 50/50, 30/70 the same month; NET n and n days n/30 months; n months; blank or unreadable, and Other, a month later, those suppliers named), a purchase keyed whole by its recent suppliers' terms weighted by their bills; the P&L layout's Salary & related lines the month after; every other line in its month; depreciation, amortisation, write-offs and stock left out; the Worker keeps an answer 60 s. The page: Cash Flow › By month › Forecast switch (by account only): each month Actual | Orders & bills | P&L forecast (Orders & bills from the current month on), the later months the forecasts alone, each forecast's Balance b/f from the month's actual b/f and then on from its own c/f.
- Cash Flow Capex & financing (`GET`/`PUT /accounting/reports/cash-flow-forecast/inputs`; `scm.acc_forecast_inputs`, one row per company, month and account, `amount_sen` + money in / − money out, never zero; `acc/forecast-inputs.ts`): the accounts offered are the active leaves the Cash Flow layout places under FUNDING IN / (OUT) (`cf:funding`), trade finance (`cf:funding:tf`) and accumulated depreciation / amortisation aside, each once, grouped by the categories that place it; the table shows the current month and the eleven after it; a PUT (write = `scm.payment_voucher.write`) replaces the company's whole table, names the first bad cell and refuses an account the layout no longer offers (the table shows those to clear); the forecast puts each amount on its own account in its own month (a past month beside its actual) and the Worker's forecast answers for the company are dropped on save. The page: Cash Flow › By month › Capex & financing (`reports/CapexFinancingDialog.tsx`).
- Cash Flow Orders & bills (`docs` in the same answer when the range reaches the current month; `acc/cashflow-docs-forecast.ts`): the money already in hand — the current month as at its 1st (an order's balance with what was paid on it since added back, orders placed since left out; the AP Aging as at the day before), each later month from today's, a year at most. An open order's unpaid balance (not CANCELLED / DRAFT / CLOSED) comes in its delivery month (amended date, else the customer's) on the debtor control's Settlement; a passed date is the current month on its own row; no delivery date is no month, listed by the year ordered (owner 2026-11-24). A supplier is paid its books balance (owner 2026-11-24 「用账本的数」: the formal AP Aging, its unapplied money on its oldest bills, so the newest are owed) on each bill's plan (Bills to pay), else its due date, else its month + the supplier's term, on the bill's own accounts (a PI's journal debits off the control, an AP invoice's lines); past due is the current month on the creditor control's own row. The Forecast P&L's other lines as the P&L forecast times them, less the bills already counted on that account that month; the Capex & financing table as keyed.
- Cash Flow trade finance (`GET`/`PUT /accounting/reports/cash-flow-forecast/tf`; `scm.acc_tf_plans`, one row per company and facility: `tenor_days` / `rate_bp` null = the default, `suppliers` jsonb array of the company's supplier ids, `off` jsonb `YYYY-MM` → ids unticked; `acc/trade-finance-forecast.ts`): a facility is an active liability leaf named TRADE FINANCE that is not a bank account (Houzs: 460-0009; 2990 none); its credits are draws (the opening balance among them), its debits repayments, which pay the oldest draws first; each owed draw is repaid a tenor after it (D3, owner 2026-11-24: 60 days, no limit; past due in the current month) with interest per draw (Finance's, else the last six repayments' interest ÷ principal) on the account the repayment vouchers debit beside the facility (Houzs 900-L004); the chosen suppliers' dues — the bills the books owe them (step 4's timing) and their Forecast P&L figures from the current month on, after their term — are drawn in their month unless unticked ("Drawdown — planned" on the facility, money in) and repaid a tenor later with their interest. The plan's legs go into both forecast columns, and the Forecast P&L's own line on that interest account steps aside. A PUT (write = `scm.payment_voucher.write`) refuses an account that is not a facility and a supplier not of the company. The page: Cash Flow › By month › Trade finance (`reports/TradeFinanceDialog.tsx`).
- Forecast Balance Sheet (`GET /accounting/reports/balance-sheet-forecast`, read = `scm.payment_voucher.post`; `acc/forecast-balance-sheet.ts`): from the actual Balance Sheet at the last month-end, each month from the current one to the last the Forecast P&L has figures for (a year at most) moved by double entries: the Forecast P&L's sales Dr debtor control, its purchases (purchase accounts) Cr creditor control, its salaries Cr the salary accrual (a liability named ACCRUAL…SALARIES, paid the month after), its depreciation and the like on a "Forecast depreciation" line beside the accumulated depreciation; the P&L forecast column's money from its own months on (customers' money Cr debtors, purchases Dr creditors, other costs and income against the profit), the trade-finance plan on the facility (its owed draws as at the start, its interest against the profit), the Capex & financing table on its accounts; the bills the books owed at the start when due (past due in the first month); the salaries owed at the start in the first month; the open orders' balances on delivery, never beyond the debtors on the start sheet (Houzs holds deposits as an AR credit, D4, so usually none). Profit on Current period earnings, cash on a "Forecast cash in / out" line beside the bank accounts, stock as at the start; each month a Balance Sheet on the same layout, its check unchanged; the Worker keeps an answer 60 s. The page: Balance Sheet › By month › Forecast switch — the current month Actual | Forecast, later months the forecast alone; forecast figures do not open.

## Rules that must not break

Engine and chart
- Only `postJournal` / `reverseJournal` (`backend/src/acc/engine.ts`) write journal lines; a new auto-posting type needs a rule in `acc/rules.ts`, a caller building lines through it, and a behaviour-lock test.
- Gate order: shape (≥2 one-sided integer-sen lines) → balance → chart (exists for the company, active, not a header) → idempotency (one ACTIVE entry per company + source_type + source_doc_no; read fails closed) → per-company number. `validateJournal` is the read-only half.
- Database backstops stay: `acc_je_balanced_totals`, `acc_jel_nonneg`, `acc_jel_one_sided`, partial unique `acc_je_one_active_source`, trigger `trg_je_balanced`.
- Account codes resolve through roles (`scm.acc_account_roles`, per company); never hardcode a code at a call site.
- `jePrefixForCompany` (`scm/lib/doc-no.ts`) reads `public.companies` via `sb.schema('public')`; it fails closed and `postJournal` returns `je_prefix_failed`.
- Which company's chart / roles / acquirers a posting reads is decided only by `accMastersCompanyId` (`acc/masters-company.ts`); never re-implement its fallback inline.
- New finance tables take the `acc_` prefix; never reuse an existing `scm` table name in a migration.
- Every finance router declares `router.use('*', supabaseAuth)` itself (pinned by `backend/tests/scmRouterBridge.test.ts`).
- Reads are company-scoped and page past the 1,000-row cap; a company id sent in a body is re-checked against the caller's grants.
- Header accounts never post (父户不记账): engine, `requireLeafAccount` and AccountSelect all refuse; an account with any sub-account, retired ones included, is a header.
- Control accounts are never hand-picked: roles AR, AR_OTHER, AP, AP_OTHER (`CONTROL_ROLES`; manual journals refuse) and special types SDC/SCC/SBS (`requireLeafAccount` refuses).
- One definition per code across companies: rename via `scm.acc_rename_account` (one transaction, collision refuses), updates hit every company, delete only a never-used code, tick OFF cascades to children, tick ON brings the parent chain.
- `section` decides the account type (`scm/lib/account-sections.ts`); moving a header moves its subtree, a child refuses (`section_child`); a reparent onto an account with postings refuses (`parent_has_postings`); parent shares the type; no cycles.
- 405-x suppliers book to AP_OTHER (405-0000), all others to AP (400-0000) — `apControlRole` only; an AP payment on the other control refuses `wrong_ap_control`.
- Lines post per item group (`acc/item-group-split.ts`): ungrouped (`line_ungrouped`) or unbound (`group_unbound`) refuses by name, never a default account. Groups are born only via `scm.acc_register_item_group`; discounts stay company-level.
- PI posts periodic: documents never touch 330-0000; stock value reaches the GL only through STOCKADJ, replayed on `inventory_movements.movement_date`.
- Migrated documents (`migrated_no_stock`) and `imported`-method payments book nothing — AutoCount already carries them.

Customer payments
- Every sales-order payment insert (panel, scan job, both SO-create inserts) goes through `bookSoPaymentBestEffort` (`scm/lib/so-payment-row.ts`): books SOPAY, issues the deposit invoice, births the official receipt; never blocks the order; logs refusals.
- AR lines carry `party_code` from `customerPartyCode`: the debtor code when kept, else the order's `customer_id`.
- A payment edit re-posts only if amount, paid date, method or merchant provider changed; the contra is dated on the original entry's day. A delete's contra is dated today.
- A payment changes only as a draft, on its keyed day, or under the amend right with a reason. A reconciled payment (confirmed settlement line, bank match on its entry, locked bank month on its money leg) is locked for everyone; each check fails closed.
- A converted payment row cannot be PATCHed; refunds plus conversions never exceed the pool (`orderMoney`, `scm/lib/so-money.ts`); converted rows are skipped by daily close, drift check and receipt healing.
- The pool is the payments the ledger booked plus, on a Sales Order, the payments brought over from AutoCount (method `imported`; owner 2026-10-05) — the panel says how much came from AutoCount (`importedSen`), and a refund or conversion of it books Dr AR like any other (the AR Aging footnote for AutoCount deposits does not grow). The Customer Refund headroom (`lib/pv-refund.ts`) reads the same; a migrated Sales Invoice stays out. The "same customer" for Convert is customer id, else phone, else debtor code AND name — never a debtor code alone (Houzs cash debtor 300-C002 is on thousands of orders). Many orders are read in one batch (`ordersMoney`), never one by one.

## Gotchas

- Reading `companies` on the scm-pinned client wrote no journals for days — always `sb.schema('public')`.
- Finance routers without their own `supabaseAuth` failed in production while harness tests passed — declare it on every new router.
- Payment inserts that skipped the hook never reached the books — use `bookSoPaymentBestEffort`; run the Self-check dry run (`POST /accounting/backfill/customer-payments {dryRun: true}`) before backfilling.
- Selecting columns `mfg_sales_orders` lacks passes the fake client — use `debtor_name` / `phone`; `soPaymentOrderColumns.test.ts` pins the selects.
- A route's response shaping dropped a computed field — assert the route reply, not only the library.
- A source type the control check does not know shows as foreign lines — add every new source type to its control's family (`SOCONV` was missed until 2026-10-06 and its two AR lines read as findings).
- `CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS` on a taken name is a silent no-op — choose a new name.
- Writing an enum column from a CASE of text literals fails (42804) — type each branch as the enum.
- Backfill contras dated on the run day overstated earlier months — pass the original entry's date.
- Counting an unconfirmed settlement link as reconciled locked correctable payments — lock only on a confirmed line.
- A fee account left on an inactive code refused every confirm unnoticed — setup offers only postable leaves and names a bad one.
- Candidate reads bound to the date window missed late-keyed payments — fetch references regardless of date; Find the sale searches everything.
- Batch detail reading only `candidates` / `suggested` showed matched lines as unexplained — fall back to `matched`; refuse an upload whose row insert returns fewer ids.
- Untagged payments walked per acquirer were counted once per merchant — list them once.
- Bank Undo left match rows behind and blocked re-matching — undo deletes them.
- The lock and the screen built different match indexes and disagreed — both use `matchesByLineOf`.
- Trial balance summed both sides of reversal pairs and drafts — filter lines before the join.
- Bank rows seeded state on mount and ignored the matcher's fresh decision — key rows on the decision (`decisionKey`).
- Header detection that read only active children let a header take postings — count retired children too.
- `fetchMonthlyDocNos` reading whole rows minted -001 twice — read the named column.
- DateField on touch: `showPicker()` from onClick is dead on iOS and a full-field overlay blocks typing — keep the native date input on the icon only.
- A card settlement confirm moves money keyed under no bank or the wrong bank: one `SETTLEMOVE` line per clearing account the payment debited, into the merchant's own clearing account (`clearingMoveLinesFrom`, `backend/src/acc/rules.ts`), and it corrects the payment's `merchant_provider` with an `UPDATE_PAYMENT` history line.
