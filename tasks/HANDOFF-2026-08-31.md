## 2. OPEN — found, root cause proven, NOT yet fixed

### 2a. A line added to a sales order makes the document un-editable afterwards

**This is the one to fix first.** PROVEN on HC-SO-013394 (probe run 33383015421):
8 lines, **7 keyed, 1 not**; two edits SENT at 07:06, then two SKIPPED with
`keyless_line`. The operator sees *"The ERP cannot tell which lines AutoCount
already has"* and "Send again" cannot clear it.

The mechanism, and why add-line only half works today: the route DECLARES the row
it just inserted (`newLineIds` → `IsNewLine`), AutoCount appends it and assigns a
DtlKey — **and nothing carries that key back**. `linked_ac_dtlkey` stays NULL, so
every LATER edit of that document hits the keyless refusal, because the
declaration is per-request and the later request did not insert anything.

`backfill-ac-line-keys.mjs` cannot repair it either: it matches against the
committed AutoCount EXPORT, and a line created in the book today is not in that
file.

Two shapes, ranked:

1. **ROOT — `/edit` answers with the keys it assigned** (ERP line id → DtlKey) and
   the drain stores them. No export, no name matching, no window in which a
   document cannot be saved. Costs a change in `AcSyncService.cs` and a deploy on
   the office host (`deploy-on-host.ps1`), which is why it has not been done in
   the same sitting.
2. **STOPGAP — fresh export + `backfill-ac-line-keys`** for the affected
   documents. Works today, needs the AutoCount host, and leaves the window open
   for the next added line. **Say it is a stopgap when offering it.**

### 2b. A typed payment row is not saved by the page's Save

PROVEN by reading `SalesOrderDetail.tsx` + the production probe: `guardUnsavedPayments`
runs on the payments card's Done and on the page's BACK button, and on **nothing
else** — `git grep -n guardUnsavedPayments` gives three sites, two of them calls.
So an operator types a payment row, presses the page Save, the document saves and
the money row is silently dropped. On HC-SO-013393 the audit log holds **zero**
payment actions of any kind, and the only payment is the imported one
(2026-08-29, v1).

Owner, asked, chose: **the page Save must COMMIT the typed rows, not merely warn.**
Not built yet.

## 3. WHAT THE DATA SAYS — measured, and his to decide

Tool: `backend/scripts/check-mrp-unreleased-demand.mjs` (read-only; runs
33381428085, 33381688123, 33382817048, 33383351168, company 1).

### 3a. Four fifths of MRP demand was never released for purchasing

- live demand lines: **14,425**
- with NO Processing Date: **11,659 (80.8%)** on **2,238 orders**
- of those, came from AutoCount: **11,659 — every single one**
- of those, no delivery date either: 11,484

The import set no Processing Date on anything, and **MRP does not filter on it**
(it is carried for DISPLAY only — see the `DemandRow` comment in `routes/mrp.ts`).
So un-released orders sit in the plan, and the page's "Proceed PO" can raise a
purchase order against them — which is what the Processing Date exists to prevent.

### 3b. 842 old orders are still demand, and 133 of them should not be

Orders older than 12 months, still counted as live demand: **4,186 lines on 842
orders**, all migrated, back to 2023-08.

Asked of AutoCount's OWN record (`ac-fidelity-so-lines.json.gz`, `TransferedQty`,
snapshot 2026-08-11):

| the book says | lines |
|---|---|
| FULLY delivered (`TransferedQty >= Qty`) | **123** |
| PARTLY delivered | 10 |
| nothing went out | 3,983 |
| our line carries no AutoCount key | 55 |
| keyed but absent from the snapshot | 15 |

So the owner's instinct was half right and half wrong, and both halves matter:
**3,983 lines really were never delivered** — that is the book's own position, and
the import only ever took what the book still called outstanding. But **123 lines
the book considers finished are still open here**, planning purchases for goods
already delivered. Those are ours to close. (Snapshot is a point in time, so 123
is a FLOOR.)

An earlier count said "only 145 have any delivery order line". That count asked a
different question — DO lines carrying the LINK back to the sales line — and
unlinked DO lines exist in this system. The `TransferedQty` join above is the one
to trust.

### 3c. Our purchase history does not go back the way the sales history does

Among open, not-fully-received PO lines (**519** of them at any age), **none is
older than 12 months, and the oldest purchase-order date is 2026-06-19**. Stated
exactly: that is a fact about OPEN unreceived lines, not proof that no older PO
was imported at all.

It still reframes the MRP screen: an old sales order shows "no supplier / SHORT"
partly because its purchase order is not in this system to be seen.

## 4. "可以洗掉重做过?" — yes, and it would not fix 3a or 3b

`docs/ac-resync-runbook.md` is a re-runnable full resync and every stage is
idempotent, so a wipe-and-redo is technically available. It does not help here,
and the reason is worth writing down: **the two findings above are properties of
the SOURCE, not of the import.** AutoCount has no Processing Date to give (it is
OUR field, pushed out as the `PDate` UDF), and the stale outstanding orders are
stale in the book. Re-importing reproduces both, exactly, and throws away
everything the ERP has done since.

What actually moves them: decide a Processing-Date rule for migrated orders and
backfill it, and close the finished orders (in AutoCount, or marked here).

## 5. Standing caveats, said plainly

- **Purchase-order add-line and purchase-order photographs have never been
  exercised against the live account book.** Code, tests and AutoCount's own
  documentation agree; nobody has watched a line or a picture appear in the book.
  Go-live smoke test, `docs/ac-resync-runbook.md` phase 5.
- **The write-back is not instant** — one drain every 5 minutes.
- **"sent" means AutoCount accepted the call**, not that anyone has looked at the
  document in the book.
